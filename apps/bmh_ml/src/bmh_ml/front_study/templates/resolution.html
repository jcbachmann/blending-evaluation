ms per run: . Particle edge = ppm3−1/3. The reclaim step is 1 m at every level, so all levels reclaim the same 60 slices. Noise: the standard deviation of one simulation, averaged over the solutions. Shift and rank correlation compare each level's means with the finest level. Kept: solutions of the L1 front that are still non-dominated at that level. Method

Reproducing

In Code/BlendingEvaluation, with an environment outside the repository (UV_PROJECT_ENVIRONMENT=/some/env uv sync --locked --all-packages; never the repository's .venv):

ENV=/some/env apps/bmh_ml/src/bmh_ml/front_study/run_resolution.sh 2

It runs verify.py (reads the stored front, about 2 minutes), resolution.py (the clouds, about 12 minutes on 14 cores), time_levels.py (the cost, on an idle machine, about 1 minute), then builds this page and the static figure out/resolution-figure-material-2.pdf (also .svg, .png). The simulator is not seeded, so every run gives new clouds; means agree within their standard error (the noise divided by √128), the noise levels within a few percent. Data: out/clouds-material-2.npz (every single simulation), out/resolution-material-2.csv (one row per solution and level), out/resolution-data-material-2.json (what this page embeds). All files are in workdir/front-interpolation/out/ (git-ignored). Documentation: apps/bmh_ml/src/bmh_ml/front_study/README.md.
