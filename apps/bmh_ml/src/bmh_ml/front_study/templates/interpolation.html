Front Interpolation Web

Each faint line joins two solutions of a simulator-optimized Pareto front. It runs through depositions on the straight line between the two deposition schedules, each point the mean of 32 simulations: 39 evenly spaced ones, and more wherever the line changes faster than simulator noise explains, down to 1/2560 of the line. Each solution is drawn at the mean of 128 single simulations, and the faint cloud around it shows them, so you can see how far simulator noise alone moves it. Hover over a solution to highlight its lines, click to pin it, then move along a line to see the schedule at that point.
