Stockpile Simulator Comparison

Six ways to simulate stacking and reclaiming, run on the same bed: 60 m long, 20 m deep, built up to an ideal 10 m pile. Material quality rises linearly from 0 to 1 over the deposition time, so color shows the order in which material arrived. Reclaiming cuts at 45° from x = 0 towards x = 60 m in 1 m slices. The time slider shows the stockpile at any moment of the stacking.
